A feel for the full festival

72 Hours at Xpressions: The Complete Festival Breakdown

08:30 AM — The Early Rush & Strategy Prep

The morning haze over the campus pathways gives way to crisp suits and hurried footsteps toward the academic blocks. Inside packed auditoriums and lecture halls, teams pace through financial models, tweak deck slides and rehearse executive pitches while judge panels assemble for flagship business simulation challenges like J.O.A.T. (Jack Of All Trades).

10:30 AM — High-Stakes Corporate Battles

Inside the halls, the intensity spikes. Real-time trading in Trade Wars (hosted by FinX), international expansion pitches in Pitch It Abroad, supply-chain strategies during Aarohan (X-Ops), and HR case study finals like Nirnay bring teams head to head.

01:00 PM — Street Culture & Open-Air Energy

The action spills across the central pathways. Dholak beats and chants draw spectators to Aaghaz, the Nukkad Natak showdown. Nearby, artists take part in Paint at XIMB while photographers cover campus for Lensation.

03:30 PM — Flea Markets & Underground Stages

Campus activity peaks around the central plaza. Food stalls, sponsor kiosks and merchandise pop-ups line the routes. In smaller open stages and seminar halls, events run back to back—from business trivia at Inquizzitive, to hip-hop at Rap It Up, and stand-up sets during Punchline.

06:00 PM — Main Stage Takeover

As dusk hits, crowds shift toward the central grounds as lighting rigs turn on. The evening opens with Carnival De Vogue, followed by rock and metal acts at Decibelz (Battle of the Bands).

08:00 PM — Stage Royale & Valedictory

The energy builds with solo and group dance competitions at Thirkan, alongside instrumental performances in Dhoon. The Valedictory Ceremony recognizes the overall champions and winners across the competitions.

09:30 PM & beyond — Pro-Nite Euphoria

The main grounds transform for headline Pro-Nites, with live EDM sets, bands and celebrity artist concerts bringing the festival crowd together.
